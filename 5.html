A Birthday Letter

A Little Message, Just for You

Dear RuHi,

Happy Birthday, Sona!

I don't know if a website can ever express everything one person wants to say to another, but I wanted to try something different this time.

I wanted to create something that you could explore slowly — a little collection of memories, thoughts, smiles and moments that mean something to me.

From the simplest conversations to the memories we have made, every little chapter has its own place. And somewhere along the way, you became a very special part of my everyday world.

I hope this new year brings you closer to everything you dream of. I hope you keep discovering new things, achieving what you work for, laughing without reasons and finding happiness in the smallest moments.

Never stop being yourself. Keep growing, keep smiling and keep becoming the person you want to be.

And whenever you revisit this little universe, I hope it reminds you of one thing: someone took the time to make all of this because your special day deserved something special.

Happy Birthday once again, Sona. With warmth and all my love, Himmu ♡
